### The exact mechanical pitfalls revealed

- **Hunters' sunlight crossbow (28869)**: the reviewed item research calls for **66 Ranged + 50 Hunter** to equip, and antler bolts (sunlight/moonlight), not generic diamond/rune crossbow bolts. **74 Fletching** relates to making it, not equipping. GC02-026/027/028 omit the Hunter gate. Source page research: `https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hunters%27_sunlight_crossbow` (latest indexed; revision unpinned in this wave).
- **Masori body (f) (27238)**: requires **80 Ranged + 80 Defence** to equip; GC02-030 recorded only Defence. 90 Crafting is an upgrade/creation requirement, not the equip gate. Source research: `https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Masori_body_%28f%29` (revision unpinned).
- **Broken state is not universal:** reviewed research distinguishes Barrows equipment at zero degradation (stats and set effect inactive) from broken Blood Moon items whose Bloodrager set effect **may** remain operative. Do not encode `broken => all effects off`. Exact per-piece and state claims still need revision pins and L2.
- **God affiliation is not cosmetic:** blessed d'hide can share offensive/defensive bonuses yet differ for GWD protection (Armadyl, Bandos, etc.). Guthix does not automatically stand in for a named God Wars god. `STATS_ONLY` equivalence must not imply `GOD_PROTECTION`.
- **Per-piece bonuses matter:** crystal bow/Bowfa armor interactions, Hueycoatl hide/bracers, Bloodbark and Swampbark do not all depend on one generic full-set flag. A set requirement only blocks when the actual mechanic/method depends on that complete set.
- **Transformation is not ownership:** imbued rings, charged glory, upgraded/recoloured Slayer helms, ornamented weapons, noted forms, bank placeholders and uncharged/broken states need exact observation and directional state edges. Bank placeholder is not stock; a noted owned item may need unnoting before use; unseen items are not zero owned.
- **Scope every relation:** `STATS_ONLY`, `EQUIP_SLOT`, `COMBAT_EFFECT`, `SET_EFFECT`, `GOD_PROTECTION`, `TELEPORT`, `ITEM_USE`, `ECONOMIC` (and narrower context/condition), not one universal `equivalent=true` flag.
