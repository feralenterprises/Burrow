# R10C artwork provenance and exclusions

`assets/scurrius-design-reference.png`, `assets/sarachnis-design-reference.png`, and `assets/bryophyta-design-reference.png` are **cropped from the owner-provided illustrative Chronicle gallery screenshot** `D10FA3A7-0C9E-44F3-94F0-E2E871644A84.jpeg` dated November 3, 2026. These images are already part of the owner's requested layout reference, but they are not verified authentic game-model renders.

Approximate pixel crops of the 864×1536 screenshot: Scurrius (172,539)-(290,663), Sarachnis (171,786)-(291,929), Bryophyta (170,1061)-(290,1190). Resized to 128×136 with Lanczos for local design comparison. The original visual reference artwork may be an artist's approximation.

These three images **may not be promoted** into `assets/verified-models`, sent to the live plugin, or described as official game-model art without separate authenticity/source/redistribution verification and owner approval.

The `Sprites` mode uses the existing OSRS sprite sources. The standalone header retains the previously approved armored raccoon. `feral-hero.png` is not bundled.
